## BuildingLink: best angle

BuildingLink is building software for condos, co-ops, HOAs and rentals, made in New York. Its partners cover accounting (Vantaca, Yardi), payments, packages, door access and smart-home devices, and an Ecobee thermostat can already open a maintenance ticket in it. No open partner program shows on its site; partnering starts with a sales call. Best angle: sell to property managers who already use it, starting with RCP, and add what BuildingLink lacks: agents that keep it in step with the accounting system, plus leak and boiler sensors that open BuildingLink tickets. Approach BuildingLink as a partner once one manager is a reference.
