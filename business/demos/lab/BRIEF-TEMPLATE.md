# <Industry> quick win: deployment brief

Keep the whole brief to about one page. Plain words, no em dashes. Mark guesses as guesses.

1. **Buyer:** who signs at a 5-50 person firm like these leads, and the two things they care about most.
2. **The quick win:** the ONE job to hand off first. Why this job: volume, pain, low risk, text in and text out, a person approves.
   Name 2 rejected alternatives and why they lost.
3. **Today:** how the job is done now, by whom, the rough steps, where the time goes (describe; do not invent figures).
4. **Deploy in week one:** exactly how it would run for a real client. What arrives where (shared inbox, web form, texts),
   how the agent gets it (e.g. a forwarding rule to a dedicated inbox), where the draft lands, how the approver approves,
   what access is needed, what is NOT touched, setup steps day by day, data and privacy rules, what happens on a bad draft.
   Keep it to tools a small firm already has. No system integration required for week one.
5. **Proof moment:** what the owner sees in the meeting that makes them say yes (the lead pastes their own message;
   which message type works best; what a great draft must get right in this trade).
6. **Risks and guardrails:** trade-specific (licensing, advice, PHI, money, promises). What the agent must never do.
7. **Pilot:** two weeks, one job. What we measure and how (before vs during), what success looks like in their words.
8. **Land and expand:** 2-3 next asks after the pilot works, each <= 60 chars, ordered by value.
9. **Variants:** which leads in leads.js need a different quick win because of their trade (feature flags), and that variant.
10. **Draft quickWins entry:** a JS object per variant, exactly matching ARCHITECTURE.md "Revision 4" schema.
